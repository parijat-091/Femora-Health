Who should have a Sexual Wellness appointment?

This appointment is for any woman who wants to have a more active, vibrant sex life. It's especially helpful for those who are experiencing:

  • Pain with sex, vaginal dryness, or libido changes
  • Sexual wellness concerns postpartum, during perimenopause, or menopause
  • Changes to their sex life which could be related to medications like antidepressants (SSRIs) or times of high stress

Symptoms we care for

We provide care for a range of symptoms, including:

  • Pain during or after sex (dyspareunia)
  • Vaginal dryness
  • Low libido or changes in sexual desire
  • Pelvic pain or discomfort
  • Difficulty with arousal or orgasm

How the Sexual Wellness appointment can help

Your provider will work with you to understand the root cause of your concerns, including the mental health components of sex and intimacy. Together, you will create a personalized care plan that can include a variety of solutions — from lifestyle recommendations like lubrication or mindfulness practices, to trusted referrals for pelvic floor physical therapy, or prescriptions for hormone support and other medications.
