What is a Perimenopause & Menopause Assessment visit?

The Perimenopause & Menopause Assessment is a comprehensive, whole-body visit with one of our menopause care specialists. We'll listen to your concerns, review your health history, and work with you to create a personalized care plan for your unique journey through perimenopause or menopause — all with your preferences at the center.
